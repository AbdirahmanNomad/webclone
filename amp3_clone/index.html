What is YouTube to MP3 Converter?

A YouTube to MP3 converter is a handy tool that allows you to extract audio from videos, playlists, and shorts on YouTube and save them in MP3 format. This means you can listen to your favorite music, podcasts, interviews, or any other audio content offline without needing access to YouTube or an internet connection. Whether you're on a long commute, traveling, or want to enjoy audio content without interruptions, a YouTube to MP3 converter is the perfect solution for creating an offline audio library. AMP3 YouTube to MP3 Converter is ad-free and designed to be simple and user-friendly. You can download high-quality MP3 files from any YouTube video or playlist with just a few clicks.

How to use AMP3 YouTube to MP3 Converter?

  1. You visit YouTube.com and find a video, short, or playlist that you would like to convert to MP3. AMP3 also works for playlists, so you can download multiple songs at once that are in the playlist. You then copy the video or playlist URL from the upper URL bar in your browser. If you are on an Android phone or an iPhone, you click the share icon below the video and then click the copy link button.
  2. You then go to amp3.cc website and paste the copied URL into the input field.
  3. Next, you select the additional conversion options:
    • Quality: You can select the quality of the MP3 file, which ranges from 64k (the worst) to 320k (the best). The default selected quality is 128k.
    • Playlist: You can enable or disable the playlist option. If you enable the playlist option and input the playlist or mix URL into the input box, the whole playlist will be converted. All songs in the provided playlist will be converted, or the conversion will stop when the 240-minute limit is reached. If the limit is reached, you can still download the playlist, but it will be cropped to 240 minutes. Only the current video will convert for single videos, even if the playlist option is enabled. If the playlist option is disabled, and you input the playlist or mix URL into the input field, only the current song will be converted.
    • Trim: You can trim the selected audio to the specified timeframe. To do this, input the starting timestamp in the first part of the trim field, from where the audio should be trimmed, and the ending timestamp in the last part of the input field, from where the audio should be trimmed. If you select the playlist option, the trim option will be disabled, as the trim only works for single video/audio downloads.
  4. After that, you click the "Convert" button, and your conversion will start.
  5. When the conversion is finished, the MP3 file or .zip file (in case you were converting the playlist) will automatically be downloaded to your computer. If the download does not initiate automatically, you can click the "Download" button. You can then click on the "Convert another" button to convert another YouTube video, short, or playlist to MP3.
